Bow to the Bulb!

In my Art-74 class, my professor had us use Minecraft to create a cultural icon. I slaved over my laptop for a full work week and then some to learn, not only how to play, but also I had to take a 2D object and turn it into pixels. I picked Pokemon's #001 Pokemon, Bulbasaur, because Pokemon has had a profound impact on my life. Bulbasaur, and other grass types, have been my go to starter since Christmas 1998.


*Bulbasaurs prefer to be worshipped by people on their knees, planting new seeds in the ground.
